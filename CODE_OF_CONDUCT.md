# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

SysWisdom.AI LLC is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community for everyone. We believe that great things happen when people from diverse backgrounds work together with mutual respect.

## Our Values

### 🤝 Collaboration
- We work together toward common goals
- We value diverse perspectives and ideas
- We lift each other up, not tear each other down

### 💡 Intellectual Honesty
- We acknowledge what we don't know
- We follow data, not ego
- We admit mistakes and learn from them

### 🛡️ Integrity
- We do what we say we'll do
- We respect each other's time and energy
- We honor confidentiality and commitments

### 🎯 Focus
- We stay focused on solving the problem
- We avoid personal attacks or dismissiveness
- We keep discussions professional and constructive

### 🌱 Growth
- We see feedback as an opportunity to improve
- We encourage learning and development
- We celebrate progress and effort

---

## Expected Behavior

### In All Interactions (Code, Comments, Issues, Emails, Meetings)

✅ **DO:**
- Be professional and respectful
- Give credit where it's due
- Listen actively and try to understand others
- Assume good intent, but address harmful behavior
- Ask clarifying questions before judging
- Be punctual and honor commitments
- Acknowledge mistakes and learn from them
- Support teammates' growth and success
- Celebrate diverse perspectives
- Keep discussions focused on ideas, not personalities

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use offensive language or slurs of any kind
- Make personal attacks or belittle others
- Engage in harassment, intimidation, or bullying
- Discriminate based on identity, background, or beliefs
- Share others' private information without consent
- Spread rumors or gossip
- Dismiss perspectives without understanding them
- Make assumptions about others' capabilities
- Monopolize discussions or meetings
- Condescend or talk down to others

## Reporting & Enforcement

### If You Witness Harmful Behavior

**Please report it immediately:**

📧 **Email**: info@syswisdom.ai  
**Subject**: "Code of Conduct Concern"

### What to Include:

- Description of the behavior
- Who was involved (if safe to share)
- When and where it occurred
- Any relevant context
- How it affected you or others
- Your contact information

### Our Response:

1. **We'll acknowledge** your report within 24-48 hours
2. **We'll investigate** thoroughly and fairly
3. **We'll keep it confidential** to the extent possible
4. **We'll take action** to address the concern
5. **We'll follow up** with you unless you prefer not to be contacted

### Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- **Warning** — First offense, clear conversation about expectations
- **Temporary restriction** — Loss of participation privileges for a set period
- **Permanent removal** — Ongoing or severe violations that harm the community

**All enforcement decisions are made by SysWisdom.AI leadership and are final.**

## Five Ways to Build a Great Community

### 1. Listen with Empathy
Try to understand where others are coming from before responding. Ask questions to clarify, not to challenge.

### 2. Assume Good Intent
Most people are trying their best. If something bothers you, ask first rather than assume malice.

### 3. Celebrate Differences
Diverse backgrounds, perspectives, and experiences make us stronger. We grow when we learn from each other.

### 4. Give Feedback Kindly
Feedback is a gift, but how we deliver it matters. Be specific, kind, and focused on improvement.

### 5. Take Care of Each Other
Look out for burnout, exhaustion, and overwhelm. Suggest breaks. Celebrate wins. Remind each other why this work matters.

## Examples

### ✅ What Good Looks Like

**Scenario**: Someone suggests a different approach to the architecture.

```
Good Response:
"That's an interesting idea. Can you walk me through your thinking? 
I want to understand the tradeoffs. Here's our current concern..."

Bad Response:
"That's a terrible idea. We have no time for this."
```

**Scenario**: Someone made a mistake in their code.

```
Good Response:
"I think there might be an issue here. Based on the test, it looks 
like [specific behavior]. Did you intend this? Let me know if you 
want to pair on a fix."

Bad Response:
"You didn't test this. This is sloppy work."
```

**Scenario**: Someone has a different perspective on data quality.

```
Good Response:
"I see it differently. Here's why I think [approach] might work 
better. What am I missing from your perspective?"

Bad Response:
"Your approach is wrong. Everyone knows [my way] is better."
```

### ❌ What Not to Do

❌ Making jokes about someone's identity, background, or appearance  
❌ Dismissing ideas without trying to understand them  
❌ Using passive-aggressive language or tone  
❌ Excluding someone from conversations or decisions  
❌ Taking conflict personally instead of staying focused on issues  
❌ Spreading rumors or private information

## Support Resources

### If You're Struggling

We understand that working on complex problems is hard. If you're feeling overwhelmed:

- **Talk to someone** — You don't have to handle this alone
- **Take a break** — Rest is productivity
- **Ask for help** — We're here to support each other
- **Reach out** — info@syswisdom.ai or your direct contact

### If You've Been Harmed

Your wellbeing matters. Please:

1. **Report it** (see Reporting & Enforcement section)
2. **Take care of yourself** (talk to trusted friends, seek support)
3. **Know it's not your fault** — Harmful behavior is never acceptable
4. **We'll support you** — We'll take your report seriously
