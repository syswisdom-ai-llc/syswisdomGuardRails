What is Drift? Model drift occurs when the patterns in production data diverge from what the model learned during training. It shows up as a decline in human-approval accuracy over time; engineers start rejecting more AI recommendations, meaning the model's understanding no longer matches real-world data.

What is Hallucination? AI hallucination in data quality happens when the model flags issues that don't actually exist (false positives). A high false-positive rate means your AI is confidently identifying problems that human reviewers consistently reject as non-issues. This wastes engineering time, erodes trust, and signals the model needs retraining.
